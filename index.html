SATB Hum-Along

Upload four-part sheet music and hear soprano, alto, tenor and bass hummed together.

Drop a PDF, a photo of a page, or a MusicXML file here

Clean PDFs exported from notation software read best. MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl) is read exactly.

How to fix wrong notes
  • Click a note to select it (you'll hear it). Click empty space to move the start point.
  • ↑/↓ change pitch by a half step (hold Shift for an octave).
  • ←/→ select the previous/next note; Shift+←/→ move it by half a beat.
  • +/− make it longer/shorter. Delete removes it. Drag notes with the mouse.
  • Double-click empty space to add a note to the part chosen in “Add/edit part”.

Each dot marks a note the reader found, coloured by part. Click one to select it in the editor. Missing dots or wrong colours show where to fix things.

The reader guesses these from the page. If the music sounds wrong throughout (every F a half step off, parts swapped, a part an octave out), correct them here and re-read. Re-reading discards edits made in the Notes tab.

Everything runs in your browser; your music is never uploaded anywhere.
